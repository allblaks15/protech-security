Common questions

How much do security guards cost in Kenya?

It depends on how many guards you need, the shift pattern (day, night or 24 hours), whether the post is armed, where the site is, and any equipment such as radios, dogs or alarms. We visit your site for free and give you a fixed monthly price in writing before you sign.

Are your guards vetted and trained?

Yes. Every guard is screened before we hire them, including a Certificate of Good Conduct and reference checks. They complete our training course and are introduced to your site before their first shift.

Do you provide armed security?

Yes. Armed guarding and armed escorts are provided with licensed armed officers, in line with Kenyan law, and planned around a written risk assessment.

Do you work outside Nairobi and outside Kenya?

We cover Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret and other towns across Kenya, and we take on security contracts in Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, South Sudan and the wider region.

How quickly can you start?

Standard guarding can usually start within days of agreeing the contract. For urgent cover after a break-in, or for a last-minute event, call our control room on 0725 310 112.

Do I have to give a budget when I request a quote?

No, the budget field is optional. If you share a range, we can design a solution that fits it rather than quoting for something you can't use.

Tell us what you need to protect

We'll visit your site, assess the risk and send a written quote. The assessment is free.
