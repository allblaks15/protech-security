About Protech Security

A Kenyan security company built on trained people, visible supervision and straight answers.

Who we are

Protech Security is a private security company based in Nairobi, serving homes, businesses, NGOs, event organisers and project owners across Kenya and East Africa.

We started with a simple idea: a guard at the gate is only as good as the training behind them and the supervisor checking on them. So we recruit carefully, train every officer before deployment, and back every site with a 24-hour control room and response teams.

Today we provide the full range of security services under one roof, from guards and dogs to CCTV, alarms, cash in transit, close protection, fire response and investigations. That means one contract, one account manager and one number to call.

What we stand for

Vetted, trained people

Every officer is screened, including a Certificate of Good Conduct and references, and completes our training before their first shift. Guards are paid properly and on time, because a guard who is looked after looks after your site.

Visible supervision

Supervisors carry out unannounced checks day and night. You get regular written reports, and you always know who your account manager is.

Straight answers

You get a clear written quote, with no hidden extras. If something goes wrong on your site, we tell you what happened and what we're changing.

How we train our officers

Training is where security companies cut corners. We don't. Every Protech guard completes a structured course before deployment, followed by on-site induction and regular refreshers.

  • Patrolling, access control and report writing
  • Customer care and conflict de-escalation
  • First aid, fire safety and evacuation
  • Radio procedure and control room protocol
  • K9 handler courses for our dog teams
  • Site-specific induction before the first shift
Training for your staff

Compliance and licensing

Protech Security operates under the Private Security Regulation Act, 2016. Our registration details, insurance cover and statutory compliance documents are available to clients on request.

Armed services are provided with licensed armed officers, in line with Kenyan firearms law, and every armed deployment is planned around a written risk assessment.

Where we work

Our teams cover Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Thika, Machakos, Nyeri and towns across Kenya. For project and contract work we also deploy in Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, South Sudan, Ethiopia and Burundi.
