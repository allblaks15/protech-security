Best Paying Security Companies in Kenya: What Guards Should Look For

By the Protech Security team. Published 13 November 2026

If you work as a security guard, or want to, you have probably asked which are the best paying security companies in Kenya. Pay varies a lot between security guard companies in Kenya, and the monthly figure is only part of the story. This guide explains what to check so you can spot the best paying security firms in Kenya and avoid the bad ones.

What "best paying" really means

A good employer is not just the one with the biggest number on the job advert. Look at the whole package:

  • Basic pay at or above the legal minimum for your grade and location.
  • Overtime paid for hours beyond your contract, especially on 12-hour shifts.
  • Allowances such as house, night or hardship allowances where they apply.
  • Statutory contributions actually remitted: NSSF, SHIF (the Social Health Insurance Fund that replaced NHIF) and the Affordable Housing Levy.
  • Paid on time, every month, with a payslip.
  • Paid leave and off days, as the Employment Act requires.

Minimum wage for security guards

Minimum wages in Kenya are set by Government through gazetted wage orders and are reviewed from time to time, so figures change. Check the current rates with the Ministry of Labour or your local labour office rather than trusting an old figure online. Any firm paying below the gazetted minimum is breaking the law.

Signs of a good employer

  • It is registered with the PSRA, and it registers its guards as security officers. See how to check registration.
  • You get a written contract and a payslip.
  • Your NSSF and SHIF statements show the contributions.
  • It provides uniform, boots, ID, a radio or phone and, at night, a torch, without deducting the cost unfairly.
  • It trains you before deployment. See our security training approach.
  • Supervisors visit you and support you, not just check on you.

Red flags

  • Pay that arrives late or in irregular amounts.
  • No payslip, or deductions that are not explained.
  • Statutory deductions taken from your pay but not showing on your NSSF or SHIF statement.
  • Fees charged to get the job or for uniform.
  • Endless double shifts without overtime.

For clients: guard pay affects your security. A guard who is paid fairly and on time is more alert, stays longer and is far less likely to be tempted by an insider job. Ask any firm how it can pay legally at the price it quoted.

Frequently asked questions

Which security companies pay guards the best in Kenya?

Look for PSRA-registered firms that pay at least the gazetted minimum wage, pay overtime and allowances, remit NSSF, SHIF and housing levy, and pay on time with a payslip.

What deductions should a security guard see on a payslip?

Typically PAYE where it applies, NSSF, SHIF and the Affordable Housing Levy. Check your NSSF and SHIF statements to confirm the money was actually paid in.

Do security guards need to be registered with the PSRA?

Yes. The Private Security Regulation Act, 2016 provides for the registration of private security officers, and a reputable employer will help you register.
