Top 10 Security Companies in Kenya: How to Pick the Best (2026)

By the Protech Security team. Published 13 November 2026

Search for the top 10 security companies in Kenya, the top 20 security companies in Kenya or even the top 100 security companies in Kenya and you will find plenty of lists. Very few of them explain how the firms were ranked. This guide shows you how to judge Kenya security companies yourself, so you end up with the best security company in Kenya for your site, not just the biggest name.

Is there an official list of the top security companies in Kenya?

No. The Private Security Regulatory Authority (PSRA) registers and licenses private security firms under the Private Security Regulation Act, 2016, but it does not publish a ranking. Any list of the top security companies in Kenya you read online is someone's opinion, often based on company size or advertising. Size matters for some contracts, but it does not guarantee that the guard at your gate is trained, paid and supervised.

10 things the best security companies in Kenya have in common

1. Current PSRA registration

Every firm on your shortlist must be registered with the PSRA. Ask for the certificate and confirm it with the Authority. Our guide to the list of registered security companies in Kenya explains how.

2. Guards vetted before deployment

A Certificate of Good Conduct, a verified national ID and checked references, done before the first shift.

3. Real training

Access control, patrolling, report writing, first aid, fire safety and conflict handling, plus an induction on your site. See how we run security training.

4. Fair, on-time pay for guards

The top security firms in Kenya pay at least the legal minimum and pay on time. Underpaid guards leave, sleep on duty or get tempted. More in our article on the best paying security companies in Kenya.

5. Unannounced supervision, day and night

Ask how often supervisors visit and how patrols are verified.

6. A 24-hour control room

Someone should answer the phone at 3am, every night of the year.

7. Response vehicles near you

For alarm and rapid response, what matters is where the vehicles are parked, not how many the company owns.

8. Technology that is actually monitored

CCTV, alarms and panic buttons are only useful when they are linked to people who act.

9. Insurance

Public liability cover and work injury cover for guards, with proof on request.

10. A clear written contract

Fixed price, notice period, and what happens when a guard is absent.

Quick test: ask every firm on your shortlist the same ten questions in writing. The best security companies in Kenya answer all of them without hesitation.

Well-known Kenya security firms

Names you will see on most lists of top security companies in Kenya include G4S Kenya, KK Security, SGA Security, BM Security, Securex, Wells Fargo Kenya, Radar Security and Riley Falcon Security. This is not a ranking and we make no claims about any of them. Alongside the large firms there are many mid-sized security companies Kenya-wide, including Protech Security, that compete on supervision and service rather than size. Whichever firms you consider, check each one against the ten points above and confirm its PSRA status.

Top 20 or top 100: is a bigger list better?

Not really. A list of the top 100 security companies in Kenya is useful only as a starting point. What you need is a shortlist of three to five firms that cover your area, offer the services you need, and can show you their registration, training records and insurance. Three good written quotes tell you more than a hundred names.

How to shortlist top security firms in Kenya in 5 steps

  1. Write down what you need: number of posts, day or night, armed or unarmed, dogs, CCTV or alarms.
  2. Pick three to five firms that operate in your county or town.
  3. Confirm each firm's PSRA registration.
  4. Ask each firm for a site assessment and a written proposal.
  5. Compare proposals line by line: guard pay, supervision, response and contract terms, not just the monthly total.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best security company in Kenya?

There is no single best security company in Kenya for every client. The best one for you is PSRA-registered, covers your area, vets and trains its guards, pays them fairly, supervises them at night and gives you a clear written contract.

Who ranks the top security companies in Kenya?

Nobody officially. The Private Security Regulatory Authority registers and licenses firms but does not rank them, so online lists of the top 10 or top 20 security companies in Kenya are opinions.

How do I check that a security company is legitimate?

Ask for its PSRA registration certificate, confirm it with the Authority, and ask for proof of insurance and guard vetting before you sign.
