List of Registered Security Companies in Kenya: How to Check PSRA Registration

By the Protech Security team. Published 13 November 2026

Before you hire a guard, you want to know the firm is legal. That is why so many people search for a list of registered security companies in Kenya or a list of registered private security companies in Kenya. This guide explains where that information comes from, how to check PSRA registered companies, and what registration does and does not tell you.

Who registers security companies in Kenya?

The Private Security Regulation Act, 2016 created the Private Security Regulatory Authority (PSRA). The Authority registers private security firms, licenses them, and registers individual security officers. Operating a private security business without registration is an offence under the Act.

Where to find the list of PSRA registered companies in Kenya

  • The PSRA itself. The Authority's official website, psra.go.ke, is the place to start, and you can contact the Authority directly to confirm whether a firm is registered.
  • The company. Any registered firm can show you its PSRA certificate. Ask for a copy and check that the name matches the company on your contract.
  • Industry bodies. Many established firms belong to industry associations, which can be a useful extra signal, but membership is not the same as registration.

Be careful with copied lists. A security companies in Kenya list on a blog or directory may be years out of date. Registration status changes, so always confirm with the PSRA rather than relying on a third-party list of security firms in Kenya.

How to check a security company in 5 minutes

  1. Get the firm's full registered name and PSRA certificate.
  2. Confirm the registration with the PSRA.
  3. Check that the name on the certificate matches the name on the quote, invoice and contract.
  4. Ask whether the guards who will work at your site are registered as security officers.
  5. Ask for proof of insurance and the company's KRA PIN.

What registration tells you, and what it doesn't

Registered security companies have met the Authority's requirements to operate. That is the minimum. Registration does not tell you how well a firm trains, pays or supervises its guards, how fast it responds in your area, or how it treats clients. Use registration to filter your list, then compare firms on service. Our guide to the top security companies in Kenya lists the ten checks we recommend.

Red flags of an unregistered firm

  • It cannot or will not show a PSRA certificate.
  • The company name keeps changing between documents.
  • It quotes a price per guard over the phone without seeing the site.
  • The price is far below every other quote.
  • Guards have no uniform, ID card or radio.

Using a list of security companies in Kenya to build your shortlist

A long list of security companies in Kenya is only useful when you narrow it down. Keep the firms that are registered, operate in your area and offer the services you need, such as manned guarding, guard dogs or CCTV. Then ask three to five of them for a site assessment and a written quote. If you are in the capital, see our guide to security companies in Nairobi.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I get a list of registered private security companies in Kenya?

Start with the Private Security Regulatory Authority (PSRA), which registers and licenses private security firms. Confirm any firm's status with the Authority rather than relying on old lists online.

What does PSRA registered mean?

It means the company is registered with the Private Security Regulatory Authority under the Private Security Regulation Act, 2016, which it needs in order to operate legally as a private security provider in Kenya.

Are security guards registered too?

Yes. The Act also provides for registration of individual private security officers, so ask the firm whether the guards assigned to your site are registered.
