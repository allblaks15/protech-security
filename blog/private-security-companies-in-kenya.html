Private Security Companies in Kenya: What They Do and How They're Regulated

By the Protech Security team. Published 13 November 2026

Private security companies protect most of Kenya's homes, offices, banks, malls, schools and construction sites. This guide explains what a private security company does, how private security firms in Kenya are regulated, how armed private security companies in Kenya operate, and how to pick the right one.

What is a private security company?

A private security company is a business that provides guarding, response, technology and related services to paying clients. In Kenya you will also hear them called security agencies in Kenya, security firms or guarding companies. They work alongside the National Police Service, not in place of it: a guard protects your premises, deters crime, controls access and raises the alarm, while the police handle arrests and investigations of crimes.

What private security companies in Kenya offer

  • Manned guarding and residential security
  • Alarm and rapid response
  • Guard dogs and K9 units
  • Close protection (bodyguards)
  • Cash in transit and escorts
  • CCTV, alarms, access control and vehicle tracking
  • Investigations and risk consulting

How private security firms are regulated

The Private Security Regulation Act, 2016 governs the industry. It set up the Private Security Regulatory Authority (PSRA), which registers and licenses private security companies and registers individual security officers. A security firm in Kenya that is not registered is operating illegally. Our guide on the list of registered private security companies in Kenya explains how to check a firm.

Armed private security companies in Kenya

Firearms in Kenya are tightly controlled by law, and armed security is not something any firm can offer casually. Where the risk calls for it, for example at banks, for cash movements or at remote project sites, reputable firms plan armed cover around a written risk assessment, use licensed armed officers and coordinate with the National Police Service. If a company offers armed guards, ask exactly who will carry the firearm, under what licence and authority, and how the deployment is supervised. See how we approach armed security.

Ask before you sign: an unarmed, well-trained guard backed by alarms and a fast response team is often more effective, and cheaper, than an armed post.

Choosing among security firms in Kenya

  1. Confirm PSRA registration.
  2. Check vetting, training and guard pay.
  3. Ask about night supervision and the control room.
  4. Confirm response cover near your site.
  5. Get a site assessment and written, fixed-price proposal.

Frequently asked questions

Who regulates private security companies in Kenya?

The Private Security Regulatory Authority (PSRA), established under the Private Security Regulation Act, 2016.

Are there armed private security companies in Kenya?

Armed security is tightly controlled under Kenyan firearms law. Where the risk calls for it, reputable firms deploy licensed armed officers and coordinate with the National Police Service, based on a written risk assessment.

What is the difference between a security agency and a security company?

In Kenya the terms are used interchangeably. Both describe a private firm providing guarding and related security services, and both must be registered with the PSRA.
