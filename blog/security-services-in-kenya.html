Security Services in Kenya: A Complete Guide for Homes and Businesses

By the Protech Security team. Published 13 November 2026

From a single night guard to a full contract for a remote project, security services in Kenya come in many forms. This guide explains each one in plain language, which ones different sites usually need, and how to choose a security company in Kenya to provide them.

Types of security services Kenya clients use

People on the ground

  • Manned guarding: trained guards for offices, warehouses, factories, hospitals and schools.
  • Residential and estate security: gate control and patrols for homes and gated communities.
  • Armed security: for banks, high-value premises and high-risk sites, planned around a risk assessment.
  • Guard dogs and K9 units: patrol and detection dogs, always with a trained handler.
  • Close protection: bodyguards for executives, diplomats and public figures.
  • Cash in transit: cash collection, banking runs and escorts.
  • Event security: planning, gates, crowd management and VIP protection.

Technology and response

  • CCTV installation and monitoring
  • Alarms and panic buttons linked to rapid response teams
  • Access control, intercoms and electric fencing
  • Vehicle and asset tracking
  • Fire safety and fire response

Advice and investigation

  • Risk assessments and consulting
  • Investigations and forensics
  • Security training
  • Full security contracts for projects and remote sites

Which security services do you need?

Homes: a night guard or estate guard, an alarm with panic buttons linked to response, and CCTV you can view on your phone. Many families add a guard dog.

Offices and shops: day and night guards, access control for staff and visitors, CCTV covering entrances and tills, and alarm response after hours.

Warehouses, factories and construction sites: guards with patrol verification, perimeter lighting and fencing, K9 units at night and CCTV on gates and stores.

Events: a written security plan, trained stewards, bag checks, VIP protection and coordination with police and medics.

Layer it: the strongest setups combine people, technology and response. A camera records a break-in; a guard and a response team stop it.

What affects the cost of security services in Kenya

  • Number of guard posts and the shift pattern
  • Armed or unarmed cover
  • Location and distance from response bases
  • Equipment: radios, guard tour systems, cameras, alarms
  • Contract length

The only reliable way to get a price is a site assessment and a fixed written quote. Our CCTV cost guide explains camera pricing in more detail.

Choosing a security firm in Kenya

Pick a firm that is registered with the PSRA, vets, trains and fairly pays its guards, supervises at night, runs a 24-hour control room and puts everything in writing. Read our 9 questions to ask a security company and our guide to private security companies in Kenya.
