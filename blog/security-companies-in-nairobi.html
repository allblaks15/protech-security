Security Companies in Nairobi: How to Choose a Security Firm in Nairobi, Kenya

By the Protech Security team. Published 13 November 2026

There are more security companies in Nairobi than anywhere else in the country, which makes choosing one harder, not easier. This guide covers what is different about hiring security firms in Nairobi Kenya, and how to turn a long list of security companies in Nairobi into a shortlist you can trust.

Why Nairobi is different

  • Traffic decides response times. A response car on the wrong side of Mombasa Road or Thika Road can take a long time to reach you at rush hour. Ask where vehicles are parked, not just how many there are.
  • Estates and gated communities. Many Nairobi homes share a gate, so the security plan has to work for the whole estate. See residential and estate security.
  • Mixed-use buildings. Offices, shops and apartments in one block need access control that works for tenants, visitors and deliveries.
  • Night risk. Most break-ins happen at night, when supervision is hardest. Ask how night shifts are checked.

Areas we hear about most

The Kenya security companies Nairobi residents call on most typically cover Westlands, Kilimani, Kileleshwa, Lavington, Karen, Langata, Runda, Muthaiga, Parklands, South B and C, Industrial Area, Embakasi, Kasarani, Ruaka, Syokimau and Kitengela. If your site is on the edge of the city, confirm that the firm actually has guards and response cover there.

What Nairobi clients usually need

  • Day and night guards for homes, offices and warehouses
  • Alarms and panic buttons linked to rapid response
  • CCTV you can view on your phone
  • Access control and electric fencing
  • Guard dogs with trained handlers
  • Event security for conferences, weddings and concerts

7 questions to ask security firms in Nairobi

  1. Are you registered with the PSRA? (See how to check.)
  2. Where are your response vehicles based relative to my site?
  3. What is your honest average response time in my area at night?
  4. How often do supervisors visit, and are visits unannounced?
  5. How are guards vetted, trained and paid?
  6. Who is my account manager and what reports will I get?
  7. What happens if a guard does not turn up?

What affects the price

Security companies in Nairobi Kenya price on the number of posts, shift pattern (12-hour day, 12-hour night or 24-hour cover), armed or unarmed, equipment such as radios and guard tour systems, and any dogs, alarms or CCTV. A free site assessment is the only way to get a fair, fixed figure. Be wary of a firm that quotes a price per guard over the phone without seeing your site.

Tip: ask three Nairobi security firms to assess the same site and compare their written proposals line by line.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find good security companies in Nairobi?

Shortlist PSRA-registered firms that cover your area, ask each for a site assessment and written quote, and compare them on supervision, response time, guard pay and contract terms.

How much do security firms in Nairobi charge?

It depends on the number of guards, shift pattern, armed or unarmed posts, location and equipment. Reputable firms visit your site and give a fixed written quote.

Do Nairobi security companies cover Kiambu, Machakos and Kajiado?

Many do, including suburbs such as Ruaka, Syokimau and Kitengela. Always confirm that the firm has guards and response vehicles near your site.
