Guarding and response

Manned guarding

Trained, vetted guards for offices, warehouses, factories, hospitals and schools, day or night.

Our guards control who comes in and out, patrol the perimeter and record every incident. Every post has written instructions agreed with you, and a supervisor visits unannounced so standards don't slip on the night shift.

  • Day, night and 24-hour posts
  • Gate, reception and patrol guards
  • Visitor and vehicle logs
  • Uniforms, radios and torches supplied
  • Unannounced supervisor visits
  • Relief cover for leave and sickness
