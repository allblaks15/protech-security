How our cash in transit and escorts service works

We move cash and valuables for supermarkets, fuel stations, SACCOs, schools and events. Routes and times change, and every movement is tracked from our control room from pick-up to drop-off.

What's included

  • Scheduled cash collection and banking
  • Payroll delivery
  • Escorts for high-value goods and cargo
  • GPS-tracked vehicles
  • Signed chain-of-custody records

Where we provide it

Across Kenya, including Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Thika, Machakos and Nyeri, and on contract in Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, South Sudan, Ethiopia and Burundi.

Questions about cash in transit and escorts

Who uses cash in transit services?

Supermarkets, fuel stations, SACCOs, schools, hospitals, churches, event organisers and any business that handles large amounts of cash.

How is my cash tracked?

Every movement is tracked from our control room, and a signed record is kept at every hand-over from pick-up to deposit.

Can you deliver payroll cash to a remote site?

Yes. We plan secure payroll deliveries to farms, construction camps and remote sites.
