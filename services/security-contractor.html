How our security contractor services service works

For large or remote projects we take on the whole security package: people, technology, vehicles, communications and reporting. You deal with one contractor and one accountable manager.

What's included

  • Site security management
  • Guards, K9 and response in one contract
  • Camp and convoy security
  • Perimeter technology installed and maintained
  • Deployments in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda and South Sudan

Where we provide it

Across Kenya, including Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Thika, Machakos and Nyeri, and on contract in Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, South Sudan, Ethiopia and Burundi.

  1. Free assessment
