Questions about CCTV installation and monitoring

Can I watch my cameras on my phone?

Yes. Every system we install can be set up for remote viewing on your phone or laptop.

How long is footage stored?

That depends on the recorder and hard drive size. We usually recommend at least 14 to 30 days of recording.

Do cameras keep working during power cuts?

Only with backup power. We recommend a UPS or a connection to your backup supply, and include it in our quote.
