How our fire safety and fire response service works

Most fires are stopped or lost in the first few minutes. We install detection and suppression equipment, train your staff to use it and can post trained fire responders at high-risk sites and events.

What's included

  • Smoke and heat detection systems
  • Extinguisher supply, refills and servicing
  • Fire marshal and evacuation training
  • Fire drills and fire risk assessments
  • Trained fire responders for sites and events

Where we provide it

Across Kenya, including Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Thika, Machakos and Nyeri, and on contract in Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, South Sudan, Ethiopia and Burundi.

Questions about fire safety and fire response

Do you service fire extinguishers?

Yes. We supply, refill and service extinguishers, and keep records of each inspection.

Can you train our staff as fire marshals?

Yes. We run fire marshal, evacuation and extinguisher training at your premises.

Can fire responders be stationed at our event?

Yes. Trained fire responders with equipment can be posted at events and high-risk sites.
