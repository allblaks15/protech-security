How our security training service works

We train every Protech guard before deployment, and we run the same courses for companies with their own security teams, plus short sessions that teach all staff what to do in an emergency.

What's included

  • Basic and advanced guard training
  • K9 handler courses
  • Fire marshal and first aid
  • Security awareness for staff
  • Emergency and evacuation drills

Where we provide it

Across Kenya, including Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Thika, Machakos and Nyeri, and on contract in Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, South Sudan, Ethiopia and Burundi.

How we set it up

  1. Free assessment

    We visit or call to understand your site and the risks.

  2. Written proposal

    A clear plan and a fixed price, with no hidden extras.

  3. Deployment

    People are inducted, and equipment is installed and tested.

  4. Supervision

    Unannounced checks, a named account manager and regular reports.

Questions about security training

Can you train our in-house security team?

Yes. We run guard training for companies that employ their own security staff.

Do you train staff who aren't security officers?

Yes. Short security awareness, fire safety and emergency drills are available for all staff.

Where does the training take place?

At your premises or at a venue we arrange, depending on the course.
