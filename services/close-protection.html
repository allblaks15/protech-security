How our close protection (CPO) service works

Our close protection officers plan every movement before it happens: routes, venues, hospitals and fallback options. They move with you discreetly, whether you are in Nairobi for a day or travelling across the region.

What's included

  • Single officers or full protection teams
  • Advance route and venue checks
  • Airport meet-and-greet
  • Secure drivers and vehicles
  • Travel across East Africa

Where we provide it

Across Kenya, including Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Thika, Machakos and Nyeri, and on contract in Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, South Sudan, Ethiopia and Burundi.

How we set it up

  1. Free assessment

    We visit or call to understand your site and the risks.

  2. Written proposal

    A clear plan and a fixed price, with no hidden extras.

  3. Deployment

    People are inducted, and equipment is installed and tested.

  4. Supervision

    Unannounced checks, a named account manager and regular reports.

Questions about close protection (CPO)

Can you protect visiting executives and delegations?

Yes. We provide airport meet-and-greet, secure transport, venue checks and close protection for the length of the visit.

Do you provide protection outside Nairobi?

Yes. Our officers travel with clients across Kenya and the wider East African region.

How far ahead should I book close protection?

As early as possible, so we can plan routes and check venues. We can arrange cover at short notice if needed.
